# Brag Plan: Second Voice

## What is this app?
A scam-interrupt voice agent for banks: it hears a scammer coaching a payment victim through the bank's questions, asks one gentle question, and escalates before the money moves.

## The angle
The scam's own script is the hook: "Tell her it's for a car deposit. Don't mention me." — the line a coach feeds a victim. The video then shows the real product hearing exactly that, in a real recorded run against the live AssemblyAI Voice Agent API. No invented claims: every caption is copy from the product or its recorded run.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The scammer's line in a red speech bubble: "Tell her it's for a car deposit. Don't mention me."

## Key moments (the middle)
- Title: "Second Voice. It hears the coach."
- Real footage: the coach's line is picked up by the room stream (caption: "A second voice in the room"), fraud-officer score jumps to 85.
- Real footage: the agent asks, once, gently: "Is there someone there with you at the moment?" (real agent audio)

## Outro / punchline
"Scammers coach their victims. Second Voice hears the coach." + repo link.

## User flow worth showing
Customer in a Voice Check -> coach speaks -> agent asks -> (escalation, shown in the 85 s demo video).
